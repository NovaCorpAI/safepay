Pricing

Open source to start. Pay when agents move real money.

The guardrail, the MCP server and this console are MIT-licensed and free to self-host with your own keys. The hosted service charges for decisions: one payment an agent asked SafePay to judge. A decision is what costs us money (a live-web lookup and two model calls when it is not cached; a policy block costs nothing), so the price follows the cost, and you always know what a month will cost before it ends.

Launch pricing These are the prices for the first teams, onboarded by hand; they hold for them for the first year. Everything without a Soon mark works today in the open-source release and in this demo; marked items are on the roadmap and are not billed until they ship.
