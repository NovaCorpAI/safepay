        Custom payment intent

        SafePay's own payables agent: paste an invoice

        An agent that reads invoices and pays them, with no privileges: what it reads becomes a payment that goes through the same policy, history rules, web and reasoning checks and seal as any other agent. Nemotron reads the invoice, but every field it returns must appear in the text; an invoice that talks to the reviewer, or announces new bank details, is held for a person. Try the samples in order.

        Agentic commerce: an order through the PayPal Agent Toolkit

        The agent wants to buy something. PayPal's guidance for the Toolkit's order flow is to create the order and invoke pay only after a person approves; SafePay is that approver, made automatic: the Toolkit's create_order runs only after the guardrail says ALLOW, and the buyer-approval link the Orders flow requires stays intact. Held or blocked, the Toolkit is never called.

        Mandates: authorize a budget, capture what the task costs

        An agent booking a hotel does not know the final bill. It asks for a ceiling; SafePay judges the ceiling like any payment and, on ALLOW, authorizes it with PayPal (Orders v2, intent AUTHORIZE): nothing is charged yet. Each capture is then checked on its own (the agent that opened it, not frozen, within what is left) before PayPal is called, and carries its own seal. Closing voids the rest and gives it back to the daily cap. In the sandbox PayPal's published test card stands in for the company card.

        Pending approvals

        A HOLD is not a dead end: a person decides here{{ ', in Telegram' if cfg.telegramOn else '' }} or through the MCP tools. Approve releases the payment and charges the ledger; Reject stops it. Both outcomes are appended to the audit chain as their own link.
