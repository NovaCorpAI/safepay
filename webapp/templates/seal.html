    What a seal proves
    
    
    That this payment was decided by SafePay before PayPal moved it: a written policy, a check of the payee on the live web, a check that the payment served the agent's task, and a person's approval when the rules asked for one. The record was written at that moment, hashed, signed and chained, so it cannot be rewritten afterwards without this page saying so.
    
  
    
  
    
    
    What it does not
    
    
    It does not vouch for the goods or services, and it says nothing about payments that did not go through SafePay. A payment without a seal is not necessarily fraudulent; a seal that does not verify here is worth questioning.
