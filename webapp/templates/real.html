Disclosure

What is real and what is not.

So nobody has to guess. SafePay's argument is proof, not promise; this page says exactly what that proof rests on, and what is ours by design. It is the same text as the README's "What is real and what is not", kept in step with it.

Real

  • PayPal. Every payout, order, authorization, capture, void, vault token, webhook and reconciliation goes to PayPal's sandbox API: the real API, with test money. The host is pinned to the sandbox in the configuration; nothing here has ever moved real money. Without PayPal keys the console says "PayPal simulated" and payouts are simulated, and says so on every result.
  • The checks. The web check is a live Tavily search; the reasoning check is Nemotron on Nebius. Both are cached for 24 hours and capped by a daily budget that fails closed. Without keys they are skipped, and the result says "skipped".
  • The evidence. The HMAC-signed hash chain, the Ed25519 seals and the operator's P-256 signatures (WebCrypto, non-extractable) are real keys; seals and receipts verify offline against the published key.
  • The state. Supabase holds the chain, the ledger, approvals, policies and agent keys on the hosted demo; locally it is memory.
  • The numbers. The attack benchmark and the duplicate-submission benchmark are real runs, committed with their runners and raw results, and the console reruns the attack cases live. The cost per decision is measured on those runs.
  • In production. Impulsa is a real product with real micro-businesses; SafePay's history rules run there on every supplier document. The live counters at /api/impact are aggregates with the team's test accounts excluded, and they start at zero: zeros are shown as zeros. Businesses the team owns (AgentPOS, the foundation) that record their real supplier purchases are counted apart and named as ours: real use, not traction. The AgentPOS catalog and quotes come from a real store; the USDC settlement check was run read-only against a real payment on Stellar mainnet made on September 4, 2026, before the check existed.
  • Upstream. paypal/agent-toolkit#124 is a real pull request. It is open, not merged.
  • The MCP Registry. SafePay Check is listed as com.agentposhq.safepay/check. The Meta Muse Connector Platform submission was sent on November 7, 2026 and is under review: not a listed connector yet.

Not real, by design

  • No money. Sandbox only, always.
  • The cases are ours. The 7 demo scenarios, the sample invoices and the 25 benchmark cases were written by us: realistic shapes, fictional vendors on .example addresses (the legitimate vendors carry their real domains so the directory check is meaningful). No customer data anywhere in the repository.
  • The red team's cases are the model's. "Red team: new attacks" judges what Nemotron writes that minute, validated by code but not curated; misses are shown as misses.
  • The human. Whoever clicks Approve (or answers in Telegram, or presses the kit's button) is the human in the loop. Operator actions are open on this public demo so a judge needs no setup; SAFEPAY_OPERATOR_TOKEN locks them in a real deployment.
  • The kit. safepay-kit is tested end to end against the real console with the keyboard standing in for the buttons. Its GPIO board follows gpiozero and the documented pins, but has not been run on a Raspberry Pi yet.
  • The dispute switch. The CUSTOMER.DISPUTE.* webhook path (re-read, match, freeze, chain, alert) is tested offline against a scripted dispute; no real sandbox dispute has been raised against it yet.
  • Stablecoins are validated, never moved. SafePay has no wallet.
  • AP2. Intent, Cart and Payment mandates are produced and verified on our own PayPal mandates; we are not a participant in an AP2 network.
  • Customers and revenue. None yet. The pricing is a proposal; the measured figures are costs.
